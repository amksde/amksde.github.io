---
title: "Operations Fundamentals"
---

- Networking: DNS, load balancers, reverse proxies, service discovery, CDNs, firewall/security groups, certificates, and network timeouts.
- Infrastructure: processes, CPU/memory limits, disks, filesystems, object storage, backups, restore drills, and capacity limits.
- Production practice: configuration changes, access control, runbooks, change management, incident communication, data repair, and disaster recovery.
- Know the dependencies your service needs to start, serve traffic, degrade safely, recover, and be retired.
