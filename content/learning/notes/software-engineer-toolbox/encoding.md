---
title: "Encoding & Serialization"
---

- Bits, bytes, hexadecimal, endianness, signed/unsigned integers, floating-point limits, and timestamps.
- Character encodings: Unicode code points, UTF-8/UTF-16, normalization, grapheme clusters, and avoiding lossy conversions.
- Text formats: JSON, XML, CSV, URL/form encoding; know escaping, validation, size, and ambiguity pitfalls.
- Binary formats: Protocol Buffers, Avro, Thrift, MessagePack, Parquet, and ORC.
- Compression: gzip, zstd, snappy, and the CPU/network/storage trade-off.
- Schema evolution: optional/default fields, field-number stability, unknown fields, compatibility tests, and versioned contracts.
