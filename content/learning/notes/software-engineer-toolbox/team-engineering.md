---
title: "Team Engineering"
---

- Establish working agreements for code review, ownership, on-call, documentation, releases, and decision-making.
- Share knowledge through pairing, design reviews, demos, documentation, and rotation of operational responsibility.
- Improve team systems: remove recurring friction, make common work self-service, reduce single points of failure, and leave clear paths for newcomers.
- Participate thoughtfully in hiring, interviewing, feedback, and inclusive collaboration.
