---
title: "Source Control Management"
---

Git is a collaboration and release tool, not just a place to save code.

- Understand commits, trees, blobs, refs, remotes, the staging area, and history rewriting.
- Work fluently with branch, merge, rebase, cherry-pick, revert, reflog, bisect, blame, worktree, tags, and signed commits.
- Prefer small, coherent commits and pull requests with clear intent, tests, migration/release notes, and rollback considerations.
- Know trunk-based development, short-lived branches, feature flags, release branches, semantic versioning, and conventional commits.
- Review for correctness, design, security, operability, testability, and maintainability; be kind, specific, and prompt.
- Protect main, require CI where appropriate, and never put secrets in history.
