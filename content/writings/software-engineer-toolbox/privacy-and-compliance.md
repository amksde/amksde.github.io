---
title: "Privacy & Compliance"
---

- Classify data: public, internal, confidential, regulated, and personal/sensitive data. Document owners and permitted uses.
- Apply data minimization, purpose limitation, retention schedules, deletion, access control, masking/tokenization, and safe non-production data practices.
- Understand consent, data-subject requests, audit evidence, data residency, breach notification, and vendor/processor risk at the level relevant to your product and jurisdiction.
- Treat laws and contractual obligations as requirements to verify with legal and security specialists, not assumptions to interpret alone.
