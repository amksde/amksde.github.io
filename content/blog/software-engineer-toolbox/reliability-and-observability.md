---
title: "Reliability & Observability"
---

- Define user-focused SLIs, SLOs, and error budgets. Track availability, correctness, latency, freshness, and durability—not only CPU.
- Instrument the four golden signals: latency, traffic, errors, and saturation. Use structured logs, metrics, traces, correlation IDs, and business events.
- Build actionable dashboards and alerts that detect user impact, have a clear owner, and link to a runbook. Avoid noisy threshold-only paging.
- Plan on-call: escalation, severity, incident command, communications, mitigation, recovery, and blameless postmortems with tracked follow-ups.
- Build resilience: deadlines, retries with jitter, circuit breakers, bulkheads, graceful shutdown, rate limits, load shedding, and dependency fallbacks.
- Practice restores, game days, chaos experiments, and disaster recovery. Know RTO/RPO and the difference between a backup existing and a backup being recoverable.
