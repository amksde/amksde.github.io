---
title: "Technical Leadership & Career"
---

- Take ownership from problem framing through rollout and outcomes: clarify goals, surface risks, make a plan, communicate progress, and close the loop.
- Write well: concise design docs, ADRs, incident updates, PR descriptions, proposals, and meeting notes. Explain trade-offs and make decisions discoverable.
- Influence without authority: listen, seek context, disagree constructively, align stakeholders, and make it easy for others to contribute.
- Mentor through pairing, feedback, reviews, teaching, and delegation. Increase the team's judgment and reduce single points of failure.
- Plan work: slice vertically, estimate uncertainty, prioritize by impact/risk, manage dependencies, and protect time for maintenance and reliability.
- Develop product and business judgment: user journeys, success metrics, experiments, pricing/cost, compliance, build-vs-buy, and opportunity cost.
- Maintain a career narrative: record impact, learn from feedback, deepen a specialty while retaining breadth, and pursue projects with meaningful technical and organizational scope.
