---
title: "Developer Productivity"
---

- Work fluently in a Linux shell: processes, permissions, pipes, redirects, environment variables, SSH, `find`, `rg`, `jq`, `curl`, `less`, `tar`, and `xargs`.
- Debug systems with logs, `ps`, `top`/`htop`, `lsof`, `ss`, `dig`, `nslookup`, `traceroute`, `tcpdump`, and `openssl`.
- Master your IDE: navigation, refactors, debugger, breakpoints, profiler, test runner, database client, and keyboard-driven editing.
- Automate repeatable work with scripts, Makefiles/task runners, pre-commit hooks, templates, and CI. Document the unusual and keep the routine self-service.
- Read code, RFCs, logs, query plans, and metrics critically. Use notes and small experiments to convert unfamiliar systems into working knowledge.
